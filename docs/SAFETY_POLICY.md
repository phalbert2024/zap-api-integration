# HoopConnect Safety Policy

## Mission Statement
HoopConnect is committed to fostering a safe, respectful, and inclusive community for all basketball enthusiasts. This Safety Policy outlines our community guidelines, moderation practices, and enforcement procedures.

---

## Community Guidelines

### 1. Respect and Inclusion
**We expect all users to:**
- Treat others with respect and kindness
- Embrace diversity and inclusion
- Avoid discriminatory language or behavior
- Respect different skill levels and playing styles
- Be welcoming to new players

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Hate speech or discriminatory remarks based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic
- Doxxing (sharing personal information without consent)
- Impersonation of other users or public figures

---

### 2. Safety First
**Physical Safety:**
- Always meet in public, well-lit locations
- Inform someone you trust about game meetups
- Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, don't participate
- Report any suspicious or threatening behavior immediately
- Follow local COVID-19 guidelines and health protocols

**Account Security:**
- Never share your password
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Be cautious of phishing attempts
- Report suspicious accounts or messages

---

### 3. Honest Communication
**We expect users to:**
- Provide accurate information in profiles
- Show up to games you commit to (or cancel in advance)
- Be honest about skill level
- Communicate clearly and promptly
- Give constructive feedback

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- Catfishing or misrepresenting identity
- Spam or promotional content (without permission)
- Scamming or fraudulent activity
- False reporting of other users
- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent bans

---

### 4. Appropriate Content
**Acceptable Content:**
- Basketball-related photos, videos, and discussions
- Constructive feedback and coaching tips
- Game highlights and court reviews
- Motivational and inspirational content

**Prohibited Content:**
- Nudity or sexually explicit material
- Violence or graphic content
- Illegal activities or promotion of such
- Self-harm or suicide-related content
- Misinformation or dangerous health advice
- Copyright infringement

---

### 5. Fair Play
**We expect users to:**
- Play by the rules of the game
- Be a good sport in victory and defeat
- Resolve disputes respectfully
- Honor game commitments
- Give honest skill assessments and reviews

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- Game manipulation or cheating systems
- False ratings or reviews
- Harassment over game outcomes
- Intentionally disruptive behavior
- Gaming the ranking system

---

## User Responsibilities

### Before Joining a Game
- Review the game details and requirements
- Confirm your availability
- Understand the skill level expectations
- Check the location and safety of the venue
- Communicate any limitations or concerns

### During a Game
- Arrive on time
- Respect other players and property
- Follow game rules and court regulations
- Be mindful of safety
- Resolve conflicts calmly

### After a Game
- Provide honest feedback when appropriate
- Report any incidents or concerns
- Rate your experience (optional but encouraged)
- Thank the organizer and fellow players

---

## Reporting System

### How to Report
1. **In-App Reporting**: Use the "Report" button on profiles, messages, or content
2. **Email**: safety@hoopconnect.app
3. **Emergency**: Call local authorities (911 in US) for immediate threats

### What to Report
- Harassment or bullying
- Threats of violence
- Suspicious or fraudulent activity
- Inappropriate content
- Safety concerns at courts
- Violations of community guidelines
- Technical abuse or exploits

### What Happens After Reporting
1. **Immediate Review**: Reports are reviewed within 24 hours
2. **Investigation**: Our safety team investigates the claim
3. **Action**: Appropriate measures are taken (warning, suspension, ban)
4. **Follow-up**: Reporter is notified of the outcome (when appropriate)
5. **Confidentiality**: Reporter identity is protected

---

## Moderation & Enforcement

### Automated Moderation
- AI-powered content filtering
- Spam detection
- Profanity filters (optional for users)
- Pattern recognition for suspicious activity

### Human Moderation
- Trained safety team reviews reports
- Context-aware decision making
- Appeals process available
- Regular audits of moderation decisions

### Enforcement Actions

#### Level 1: Warning
- First-time minor violations
- User receives notification
- Explanation of violation
- No account restrictions

#### Level 2: Temporary Suspension
- Repeated minor violations or moderate violations
- Account suspended for 3-7 days
- Access to app limited
- Must acknowledge guidelines to restore access

#### Level 3: Extended Suspension
- Serious violations or repeated moderate violations
- Account suspended for 30-90 days
- All features disabled
- Must appeal to restore access

#### Level 4: Permanent Ban
- Severe violations (threats, harassment, illegal activity)
- Multiple violations after suspensions
- Account permanently disabled
- All content removed
- IP and device bans may be applied
- No appeals (except in cases of mistaken identity)

### Special Cases: Immediate Ban
The following will result in immediate permanent ban:
- Threats of violence or harm
- Child exploitation or endangerment
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Doxxing or swatting
- Terrorism or extremism
- Human trafficking or exploitation
- Illegal weapons or drugs

---

## Appeals Process

### How to Appeal
1. Submit appeal via email: appeals@hoopconnect.app
2. Include your username and case reference number
3. Provide explanation and any supporting evidence
4. Appeals reviewed within 7 business days

### Appeal Criteria
- Mistaken identity
- Context was misunderstood
- False reporting
- Technical error

### Appeal Outcomes
- **Upheld**: Original decision stands
- **Modified**: Reduced penalty
- **Overturned**: Account fully restored with apology

## Minor Safety (Under 18)

### Age Restrictions
- **Minimum Age**: 13 years old (per COPPA regulations)
- **Parental Consent**: Required for users under 18
- **Age Verification**: May be requested at any time

### Additional Protections for Minors
- Cannot be messaged by users over 18 (unless mutual friends)
- Limited profile visibility
- Enhanced content filtering
- Additional safety prompts
- Parent/guardian notification options

### Recommendations for Parents
- Review the app with your child
- Set privacy settings appropriately
- Monitor activity regularly
- Discuss online safety
- Encourage reporting of concerns

- Improve user experience

## Court Safety Standards

### Verified Courts
- Community-verified locations
- Safety ratings and reviews
- Lighting and accessibility information
- Emergency contact information

### Court Safety Tips
- Visit courts during daylight hours when unfamiliar
- Bring a friend when possible
- Keep valuables secure or at home
- Know the location of nearest restrooms and water
- Have a charged phone for emergencies

### Reporting Unsafe Courts
- Report safety concerns via court profile
- Include specific details (poor lighting, broken glass, etc.)
- Photos can be attached
- Safety team reviews and flags dangerous courts
