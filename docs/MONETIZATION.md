# HoopConnect Monetization Strategy

## Overview
This document outlines the pricing tiers, features, and revenue model for HoopConnect.

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Freemium Subscription Model (Primary)
### 2. Court Partnerships & Sponsorships
### 3. Transaction Fees
### 4. Advertising (Limited)

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Free Tier
**Price**: $0/month

**Features**:
- Basic profile creation
- Search and discover local courts
- Join up to 5 games per month
- View game schedules
- Send direct messages (limited to 20/day)
- Basic player stats
- Court reviews (read only)

**Limitations**:
- Cannot host games
- No priority placement in game listings
- Basic search filters only
- Ads displayed in feed

**Target Audience**: Casual players, new users exploring the platform

---

### Hooper Tier (Standard)
**Price**: $4.99/month or $49.99/year (save 17%)

**Features**:
- All Free tier features
- **Unlimited game hosting**
- **Unlimited game joins**
- Unlimited messaging
- Advanced court search filters
- Priority game notifications
- **Create private games**
- Write court reviews
- Detailed player statistics
- Custom player profile themes
- Ad-free experience

**Target Audience**: Regular players, active community members

---

### Pro Hooper Tier (Premium)
**Price**: $9.99/month or $99.99/year (save 17%)

**Features**:
- All Hooper tier features
- **Tournament creation and management**
- **Team creation (up to 10 teams)**
- Video highlight uploads (up to 10 per month)
- Advanced analytics and insights
- Priority customer support
- Profile verification badge
- Game recording and statistics
- Court check-in rewards
- Early access to new features
- Exclusive profile badges
- **Connect with verified coaches/trainers**

**Target Audience**: Serious players, team organizers, coaches

---

### Organization Tier (Enterprise)
**Price**: Custom pricing (starting at $99/month)

**Features**:
- All Pro Hooper features
- **Unlimited teams**
- **Branded organization page**
- Bulk user management
- Custom branding options
- Advanced analytics dashboard
- API access for integration
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom tournament formats
- Sponsor placement opportunities
- White-label options (for large organizations)
- Court management tools

**Target Audience**: Basketball leagues, community centers, sports organizations, schools

## Additional Revenue Streams

### Court Partnerships
- **Court Verification Fee**: $50/year per court
  - Verified badge on court listing
  - Priority in search results
  - Detailed analytics on court usage

- **Sponsored Court Listings**: $100-500/month
  - Top placement in search results
  - Featured court cards in app
  - Banner placement on nearby game listings

### Transaction-Based Revenue
- **Court Booking Integration**: 10% commission on bookings
- **Equipment Marketplace**: 15% commission on sales
- **Event Ticketing**: 5% + $0.99 per ticket

### Advertising (Limited & Targeted)
- **Free Tier Only**: $0.50-2.00 CPM
- **Sponsored Content**: Brand partnerships with sports companies
- **Targeted Local Ads**: Gyms, sports stores, athletic trainers
- **Restrictions**: No intrusive ads, basketball/fitness related only

## Promotional Strategies

### Launch Phase
- **First 1,000 Users**: Free Hooper tier for 6 months
- **Refer-a-Friend**: 1 month free Hooper tier for referrer and referee
- **Court Ambassadors**: Free Pro Hooper for users who add 5+ verified courts

### Ongoing Promotions
- **Annual Plans**: 17% discount vs monthly
- **Student Discount**: 50% off Hooper or Pro Hooper tiers
- **Team Discount**: 20% off for groups of 5+ users
- **Seasonal Promotions**: Summer tournaments, winter leagues

### Retention Incentives
- **Loyalty Rewards**: Every 12 months of paid subscription = 1 month free
- **Activity Bonuses**: Free month for hosting 20+ games
- **Community Leaders**: Free tier upgrades for active community contributors

## Payment Processing

### Payment Providers
- **Mobile**: Apple In-App Purchases, Google Play Billing
- **Web**: Stripe
- **Commission Fees**: 15-30% (app stores), 2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe)

### Billing Cycle
- Monthly or annual options
- Auto-renewal with 24-hour notice before renewal
- Easy cancellation (no penalties)
- Pro-rated refunds within 7 days
